ShortlistedBot

Who we are

ShortlistedBot belongs to Shortlisted, a research service for B2B software companies. When AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode, Claude and others) cite a public web page as a source, ShortlistedBot may visit that page once to read its text and record which software products it mentions.

How it behaves

  • User-Agent: ShortlistedBot/1.0 (+https://aravinth01.github.io/bot)
  • Reads and obeys robots.txt before every visit, including Crawl-delay.
  • Plain page requests only: one request at a time per site, at least 5 seconds apart. No logins, no forms, no proxies, and no attempts to get around blocks.
  • Pages that answer 401, 403 or 429 are never retried automatically.
  • Raw HTML is not stored; only the readable text needed to check product mentions.

How to opt out

Add this to your site's robots.txt and ShortlistedBot will stop visiting:

User-agent: ShortlistedBot
Disallow: /

Or email us and we will exclude your domain.
